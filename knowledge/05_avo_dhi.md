# AVO, Vp/Vs and direct hydrocarbon indicators (DHIs)
- Near, mid, far and ultra-far stacks image the same reflector at increasing angles.
- Gas sands in young, soft clastic basins often brighten with angle: far amplitude stronger than near.
- Low Vp/Vs and low P-impedance relative to surrounding shale point to sand and especially gas.
- Best DHIs: amplitude conforming to structure, flat spots (fluid contacts), consistent anomaly on several attributes.
- Always calibrate cut-offs at wells with known results; textbook values vary by basin and depth.
