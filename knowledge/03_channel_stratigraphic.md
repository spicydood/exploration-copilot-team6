# Channel and point-bar sands (stratigraphic trap)
- Ancient rivers leave long, winding (sinuous) sand bodies surrounded by mud; point bars sit on the inside of bends.
- Evidence on maps: elongate, sinuous high sand-probability or bright-amplitude body.
- Trap forms where the sand pinches out up-dip into shale, or where a mud-filled abandoned channel cuts it off (up-dip seal).
- Main risks: sand connectivity along the channel, thin sand below seismic resolution, lateral seal quality.
