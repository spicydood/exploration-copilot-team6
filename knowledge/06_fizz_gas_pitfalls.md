# Pitfalls: why bright anomalies fail
- Low-saturation ("fizz") gas gives almost the same AVO and Vp/Vs response as commercial gas.
- Lithology effects (coals, carbonate stringers, very clean wet sands) can also brighten amplitudes.
- Tuning of thin beds can create bright amplitudes without hydrocarbons.
- A dry well inside a bright body usually means charge, seal or saturation failed, not that the attributes lied.
