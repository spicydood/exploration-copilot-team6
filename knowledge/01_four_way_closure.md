# Four-way dip closure (structural trap)
- Layers fold up into a dome; oil and gas, lighter than water, collect at the crest.
- Evidence: contours on the reservoir-top map close around a high; the lowest closing contour is the spill point.
- Strong support: hydrocarbon anomaly whose edge follows a structural contour (conformance), or a flat spot.
- Main risks: closure measured in time may disappear after depth conversion; top seal must hold.
