# Fault trap / fault-dependent closure (structural trap)
- A reservoir is cut by a fault and sealed against low-permeability rock on the other side.
- Evidence: sand body ends sharply at a mapped fault; up-dip side of the body is the fault.
- Seal depends on juxtaposition (sand against shale seals, sand against sand may leak) and fault-rock (shale gouge).
- Main risks: fault leaks; fault re-activation; small compartments.
